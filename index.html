Comparison, overlap & statistics

Independent proteome analyses

Compare AAs

Each AA uses its own lowest-scoring successful pocket after the comparison tab’s P2Rank/pLDDT filters. Only proteins with finite scores for both selected AAs are paired; missing results are not zeros. The two best pockets can differ. Table search, preference and minimum Δ affect the candidate list and its download, not the paired-score overview. Selecting the same AA in the other selector swaps the pair.

Δ(X vs Y) = score(Y) − score(X)Positive favors X; negative favors Y. Raw score differences compare predictions, not measured affinities.
Top-hit membership & overlap

For each selected canonical AA, apply QC first, retain one best pocket per protein, then sort by score (ties by UniProt ID). Rank percentile is 100 × (rank − 1) / (N − 1), or 0 for one protein. A top-tier set includes percentiles ≤ 1, 5 or 10. This can yield more than the nominal fraction in small datasets. “Only” refers to top-list membership, not exclusive binding. Missing or QC-failing results remain explicitly unknown.

Jaccard(A, B) = |A ∩ B| / |A ∪ B|Empty unions are undefined. Venn counts are exact, but circle areas are schematic. UpSet bars represent exact membership patterns; up to 20 largest patterns are drawn and all patterns remain available in the table selector.
Competitive AAs in overlap results

The column reports x/20: the number of all 20 canonical AA top-hit lists containing the protein, using the overlap tab’s score, top-tier and P2Rank/pLDDT settings. Each AA is ranked independently after QC and best-pocket selection. For example, 8/20 means the protein is a top hit for eight AAs, even if only ALA and LEU are selected for overlap. The selected AAs still determine the table’s protein list and intersections. D-AA controls are excluded.

This is predicted top-hit membership, not proof of binding, and differs from Explorer’s score-tolerance-based Competitive AAs metric. Missing/QC-failing results remain unknown: the denominator stays 20, with incomplete eligible coverage noted beneath the count. Hover for qualifying AA names and coverage. Downloads include the all-AA count, denominator, eligible coverage and AA names, plus separate selected-AA membership counts.
Statistics population

Statistics uses its own AA, score, QC and best/all-pocket controls, independent of Explorer. All-AA mode pools the 20 canonical AAs, excluding D-AA controls; repeated proteins across AAs are not independent observations. Protein counts are unique UniProt IDs; pocket counts are unique protein × pocket IDs across selected AAs. The pockets-per-protein distribution counts distinct retained pockets for each eligible protein × AA, excluding missing/failed pairs rather than assigning zero.

Score and quality distributions follow the observation-unit selector. Percentiles, top-tier cutoffs, median selectivity and L/D statistics always use one independent best QC-passing pocket per protein × AA. Selectivity uses available canonical AAs and requires at least one comparator.
Summary statistics & correlations

Mean and population SD use all finite observations in the displayed population; quartiles use linear interpolation. Score/quality correlations pair values on the same selected pocket row. Pearson measures linear association; Spearman uses average ranks for ties. Both exclude non-finite pairs and are undefined for fewer than two pairs or zero variance. L/D pairs use independent best pockets after QC.

Histograms use all finite observations (40 bins, with integer bins where suitable). The Statistics correlation plot draws every finite paired observation after the selected QC filters, AA selection and observation unit, without a display cap. Overlapping points can share the same position. Compare AAs retains its 2,000-point display sample; correlation coefficients use all pairs in both tabs. These summaries describe the retained docking results, not excluded pipeline failures or experimental binding.

Score definitions

Scores, direction and best-pocket rule

Lower is better ↓
Vina score

Independent best Vina pose score within this pocket, stored as vina_affinity in the compact L/D files (originally vina_<aa>_affinity). More negative values indicate a stronger predicted fit. It may describe a different pose from the SFCT result.

SFCT score

OnionNet-SFCT value sfct_score for the pose selected upstream by Combined 80%. It is not an independently selected minimum-SFCT pose. Lower is treated as better.

Combined weights

Combined 80% keeps the supplied vina_sfct_combined score (80% SFCT), including its original precision.

0.2 × origin_score + 0.8 × sfct_score

Combined 50% is calculated in the app from the same saved pose.

0.5 × origin_score + 0.5 × sfct_score

Best pocket: for each protein, amino acid and selected score, the pocket with the lowest score is retained for protein ranking and all within-protein specificity calculations.

Separate score sources: the pipeline exports origin_score as sfct_vina_score. Both Combined variants use the SFCT-selected pose's original score, never the independent Vina minimum. Combined 80% remains unchanged; Combined 50% requires successful SFCT status and finite sfct_vina_score and sfct_score, even if independent Vina failed. Missing inputs produce a missing 50% score, not zero. These rules apply to both L and D data throughout rankings, filters, plots, QC, GO analysis and downloads.

Pose-selection limitation: Combined 50% reweights each pocket's saved Combined-80%-selected pose. Protein rankings can select a different best pocket under 50%, but the app cannot reselect a different pose within a pocket because other pose scores are absent. This is retained-pose reweighting, not a fresh 50/50 docking run or an independently optimized 50/50 pose benchmark.

All rankings, percentiles, AA normalization, GO backgrounds and Control QC populations are calculated within the selected organism. Switching organisms reloads the atlas, clearing selected proteins, pockets, annotations and viewer caches. Missing optional data show Unavailable; another organism's data are never substituted.

Compact data: the app reads At_results/L/manifest.json and the optional At_results/D/manifest.json, each referencing its own pocket metadata and AA score files. L and D pocket IDs are resolved within their own bundles. The earlier single-folder format remains supported when neither split manifest exists. Compression removes repeated metadata, not observations: original score precision and separate source statuses are preserved. No additional quality filters or score calculations are applied during export. The source definitions above remain unchanged.

Missing results: Vina requires vina_status = success; SFCT and Combined require sfct_status = success. A source file's generic status, when present, takes precedence for that source only. Each protein × AA × pocket is checked independently: failed SFCT does not remove successful Vina, failed MET does not remove successful LEU, and a failed pocket does not remove another successful pocket. Blank or non-finite scores are missing for that metric, not zero. Tables and calculations require a successful finite value for the selected metric; unavailable AAs appear as Missing in the protein profile.

Derived values

Ranks, thresholds and selectivity

Proteome rank and percentile

Successful finite-score pockets first pass the selected P2Rank/pLDDT thresholds. Proteins are then ordered by their best passing pocket for the selected AA and score type. Rank 1 is best; tied protein scores are ordered by UniProt ID.

percentile = 100 × (rank − 1) / (N − 1) 0% is best; the Top 1%, 5% and 10% filters retain percentiles at or below that cutoff. N is the number of QC-qualified proteins for that AA and score; a singleton has percentile 0. Inspecting a pocket places its score among other proteins' best QC-passing scores, excluding its own protein's best score. The same tie rule applies, so inspecting the best pocket preserves its percentile.
AA rank within a protein

The target AA’s rank after sorting the best score for each of the 20 canonical AAs. Rank 1 is the most favorable predicted AA for that protein.

Competitive AAs

Number of the available other canonical AAs whose best QC-passing score is within the selected score tolerance of, or better than, the target. The same quality thresholds apply to every AA. The displayed denominator is the number of available other AAs.

other score ≤ target score + tolerance D-AA controls are excluded from this count.
Δ to median

Difference between the median successful best score of the available other canonical AAs and the target AA’s successful best score.

Δ = median(available others) − target Positive values favor the target AA.
Selectivity Z-score

Target separation from the mean of the available successful other AAs, measured in their population standard deviations.

Z = [mean(available others) − target] / SD(available others) Positive values favor the target. Z is missing with fewer than two other AAs or zero standard deviation; Δ and competitive-AA count are missing with no other AAs. Missing targets have no selectivity statistics. An active selectivity filter requires the corresponding statistic to be available.
AA-normalized Z-score · profile plot only

The AA-normalized Z-score checkbox beside Value type normalizes the selected raw score (Combined 80%, Combined 50%, Vina or SFCT), independently of Explorer’s Ranking score. Unchecked, the plot shows raw values. Choosing a percentile option unchecks and disables normalization. Each AA’s reference contains one best successful, finite-scoring pocket per protein after the shared P2Rank/pLDDT filters. Search, proteome tier and specificity filters do not restrict this reference; D-AA controls are excluded.

Z = (score − mean for this AA) / population SD for this AA

Lower is better: negative values are more favorable than typical for that AA, and the reference line at zero is the AA-specific mean, not a significance cutoff. When inspecting a pocket, its score uses the same best-pocket reference distribution, including that protein’s best pocket. Hover for the raw score, reference mean, SD and protein count.

The Z = 0 line marks the mean. Dashed purple segments show each AA’s actual Top 5% score boundary in Z units: (Top 5% boundary score − AA mean) / AA SD. The boundary is the last score included by the existing proteome rank-percentile ≤ 5% rule in that AA’s QC-passing reference. This uses observed scores, not a normal-distribution assumption; different AAs can have different cutoff heights. Inspected pockets keep the same reference boundary. Tier colors retain the existing rank-based handling of ties and inspected pockets, so identical boundary scores need not have identical tier membership.

Z is missing with fewer than two reference proteins or zero score variation. This adjusts AA-specific score baselines, not true binding affinities or probabilities. It differs from Explorer’s within-protein Selectivity Z (positive favors the target). Raw table values, AA ranks and selectivity calculations are unchanged.
L>D · L versus D amino-acid controls

The 19 D-AA datasets are separate controls, excluded from the canonical 20-AA profile, AA rank, median, Z-score, competitive-AA count and matrix. Glycine has no distinct L/D pair.

L/D Δ = D-AA score − L-AA score

The L>D checkbox means stronger predicted preference for the L-AA, not a numerically larger score. Since lower scores are better, it keeps proteins with L score < D score, equivalently D score − L score > 0, for the selected AA and ranking metric. Ties and missing/failed comparisons do not pass. It is off by default and unavailable for glycine or an unloaded D-AA dataset.

Explorer and GO use the same independent best-QC-passing-pocket comparison; both L and D pass the tab's P2Rank/pLDDT filters before best-pocket selection; the D comparator is not tier-filtered. In GO, L>D filters selected proteins only, not either background option. Enrichment is recalculated for those selected proteins. “Use Explorer settings” also copies L>D; otherwise the two tabs keep independent settings. GO downloads record the selection's require_l_preference flag.

Positive favors L; negative favors D; zero is a tie. Both scores must be successful and finite. Explorer applies the same quality filters to both configurations before choosing each independent best pocket. All pockets retains this protein-level difference. An inspected profile pocket compares matching L/D pocket geometry only. Missing scores are unknown, never zero or evidence of L preference. D docking is a stereochemical comparison, not a proven nonbinding control or an FDR estimate.
Combined 80% / 50% tolerance 0.10 each Vina tolerance 0.25 SFCT tolerance 0.15

Pocket details table

Pocket-level columns

Inspected pocket and model identity: opening an Explorer pocket or pressing Inspect applies that same pocket to the structure panel, AA comparison and rank table. Cross-AA comparisons require matching UniProt ID, receptor model, pocket label, residue set and center coordinates. Mismatches, unsuccessful scores and QC failures stay missing; no other pocket is substituted. Protein profile and AA matrix share Explorer's quality thresholds, shown above their results; profile links from other analyses carry their source thresholds.

Version-pinned structure: the viewer fetches the AlphaFold model identifier recorded in the result, including its fragment and version. An unsupported or unavailable model produces an explicit message, without fallback to the current AlphaFold release. pLDDT is the default protein coloring. This pins the source model but does not verify coordinate changes made during receptor preparation. The implementation uses the versioned Mol* structure-loading API.

Pocket visualization: the Protein toggle switches between a cartoon (default) and a semi-transparent molecular surface (45% opacity), computed from the same receptor atoms. The surface is built on first use and cached for the currently loaded model. Green pocket override (on by default) colors the selected pocket’s residue_ids green on both representations, matching chain and residue number. Turn it off to see the selected protein coloring inside the pocket. Show sticks (off by default) independently shows/hides green bond cylinders; disabling either control does not disable the other. Changing pockets replaces the green region rather than accumulating highlights. Style, color and highlight changes preserve the viewing angle and other layers. The exported ligand remains element-colored ball-and-stick; P2Rank point colors are controlled separately. The protein surface is distinct from that point cloud.

Protein residue colors: Color offers pLDDT (confidence), Residue charge, Residue polarity, and None (uniform gray), independently of Cartoon/Surface. Residue charge is a qualitative classification by the model’s residue name: ASP/GLU red (typically negative), LYS/ARG blue (typically positive), HIS ochre (protonation-dependent), and other/unknown residues gray. It does not calculate actual protonation, pKa, terminal charges, atomic partial charges, or electrostatic potential; local environment and pH can change real residue charges. These colors are descriptive only and do not alter docking scores, rankings or QC. The optional green pocket layer overrides these colors only at the selected pocket; turning it off restores the underlying scheme. Missing pockets do not remove the selected protein coloring.

Residue polarity: Blue = polar (ARG, ASN, ASP, CYS, GLN, GLU, GLY, HIS, LYS, SER, THR, TYR); muted tan = nonpolar (ALA, ILE, LEU, MET, PHE, PRO, TRP, VAL); gray = other/unknown or modified residues. This uses the canonical-residue grouping from ProDy’s polar/hydrophobic selections. Polarity groupings vary between conventions, particularly for GLY/CYS/TYR; their inclusion here is a display convention, not a measured property of the local environment. Charged residues are included in the polar group. This does not calculate hydrophobicity, solvent exposure, hydrogen bonds or electrostatic potential. Pocket sticks remain green, the ligand keeps element colors, and the separate pocket-point color setting is preserved.

P2Rank point cloud: Show pocket cloud (on by default) loads At_results/pocket_points.tsv.gz only when a profile needs it, then caches the shared table. Its pocket_id joins to the matching compact L bundle; points contains semicolon-separated X/Y/Z triplets. Every exported point for the selected pocket is shown at its original position as a small sphere (gold by default; radius 0.55 Å, opacity 30%), without bonds, smoothing or subsampling. These are P2Rank surface samples, not ligand atoms, an experimentally measured volume, or a reconstructed pocket mesh. The displayed count is the actual number of exported points; when it differs from sas_points, the metadata count is shown separately, without padding or discarding points. Missing or malformed points leave the other layers visible. The approximately 21 MB first download runs outside the structure-update queue. Cloud visibility does not change any scores, filters, ranks or QC calculations. Point IDs and receptor coordinates must originate from the same P2Rank/compact export; the coordinate-only table itself contains no model identifier or checksum. Electrostatic coloring additionally validates the point coordinates against the electrostatics summary.

Pocket-point electrostatic potential: Pocket-point color offers Gold (default) or precomputed receptor potential sampled at each P2Rank point. The manifest fixes red → white → blue at −5 → 0 → +5 kT/e, following the APBS visualization convention. Only display colors saturate beyond ±5; underlying values are unchanged. This is not a docking score, ligand interaction energy, binding probability, or protein-surface calculation in the browser. Protein coloring, green pocket sticks/override, ligand, cloud geometry/opacity and camera remain unchanged when switching this selector.

Potential validation and missing data: The compressed v1 manifest and pocket summary in At_results/electrostatics load only on demand; only the selected model’s potential table is fetched, with at most two such tables cached. The app verifies the exact compact L manifest, its pocket-export hash, recorded model and pocket rank, successful calculation status, point counts, contiguous zero-based indices, coordinate-order fingerprint (SHA-256 of fixed-three-decimal X/Y/Z triplets), and compressed summary/point-file checksums. Failed, missing or mismatched results show an explicit unavailable message and gray points, not zero potential. Individually blank samples remain gray; white represents a valid zero. The browser requires HTTPS or localhost for checksum validation. Model identity and point alignment are verified, but prepared-receptor PQR coordinates are not independently compared with the displayed AlphaFold model. Electrostatic colors are visual context only and do not change any scores, ranks or QC.

Docked ligand overlay: Show ligand displays the selected L-AA’s coordinates from At_results/L/aa_positions/positions_<aa>.tsv.gz. Control QC

Control recovery and pocket-site coverage

Controls come from annotations/arabidopsis/strict_WT_single_protein_AA_controls.tsv. Only control_eligible = 1 (or true/yes) and canonical AAs are included, with one experimental representative per AA × UniProt accession. Duplicate pairs are rejected rather than counted twice. Accessions match exactly; isoforms are not merged. Each pair contributes once, so a protein binding several AAs can contribute several controls.

For each AA and each score independently, successful finite pocket scores must pass the Control QC tab’s P2Rank and pocket mean pLDDT thresholds (default 0.7 and 90) before selecting the lowest-scoring pocket per protein. In Best-scoring pocket mode (default), the control uses this pocket too. In both modes, the entire passing best-pocket proteome for that AA is the percentile reference, not just the controls. Top proteome percentile = 100 × (rank − 1)/(N − 1), with a singleton assigned 0; equal scores are ordered by UniProt accession, consistent with the other Statistics analyses. Combined 80% uses the supplied vina_sfct_combined value; Combined 50% uses the saved pose’s 50/50 origin/SFCT average, not the independent Vina minimum.

The curves are empirical cumulative recovery: 100 × controls at or below the percentile cutoff / selected denominator. By default, the same controls with eligible results for all four scores form the denominator for every curve. “All known controls” keeps missing, failed or QC-excluded controls in the denominator but never invents a rank for them; each metric can recover its own available controls. Unmatched sites are also missing, not substituted with another pocket. Coverage and exclusions are reported separately. Top 1%, 5% and 10% use inclusive cutoffs and show both percentages and counts. The dashed y = x line is an ideal random-ranking reference; finite rank grids and score ties can cause small deviations. In all-known mode, missing coverage limits achievable recovery, so y = x is not a coverage-adjusted null. These are descriptive controls, not independent validation or proof of binding.

Pocket selection applies to curves, tier summaries, analyzed-site coverage and exports. Experimental matching is performed independently of docking scores and quality filters. Experimental sites use UniProt_contact_positions with a confirmed contact_overlap mapping, never PDB author residue numbers. Each representative site is evaluated against single-chain-A AlphaFold F1 pocket residue lists in matching UniProt numbering. Pockets from all loaded AAs are deduplicated by protein/model/pocket/residue list; pockets are not merged into a synthetic site. Unsupported numbering and unavailable residue lists are reported as unknown.

Control-matched pocket mode selects the predicted pocket with the largest number of mapped experimental contact residues. Equal overlaps are resolved by lower P2Rank rank, then model/pocket/residue identity—not docking score. The chosen pocket must meet the site-overlap threshold (default ≥50%; any residue, 25%, 75% and 100% are also available). This geometry match is fixed before applying target-AA docking status, finite-score and quality checks. All four metrics use that same pocket; an unavailable score, failed QC or unmatched site stays missing. No alternative pocket is substituted. Matching is a residue-overlap approximation because the control list does not contain predicted pocket IDs.

In control-matched mode, each metric places the matched pocket’s score against the other proteins’ best QC-passing scores. Its percentile is 100 × number of other proteins preceding that score / (N − 1), using the same UniProt tie-break (singleton 0). The control’s own best score is excluded so it is not counted twice. This reference is held fixed and other controls are not replaced simultaneously. It is not a rank among controls or among all pocket rows. Control-matched mode uses experimental knowledge, so its curves describe known-site-conditioned scoring, not independent end-to-end pocket selection; y = x remains an illustrative reference.

Analyzed-pocket site recovery evaluates the actual pocket supplying each metric’s QC score. A capture meets the selected overlap threshold. Each metric’s site denominator includes controls with a usable QC-passing score and comparable residues for that analyzed pocket; Evaluable / known shows the coverage, and missing/unmatched controls are not assigned zero overlap. This denominator is separate from the curve’s Recovery denominator selector. In control-matched mode the threshold is already enforced when selecting the pocket, so 100% capture among evaluable matches is expected and is not an independent recovery test.

L versus D controls: the separate summary uses the controls in the chosen recovery denominator, excluding glycine because it has no L/D pair. For each metric, D score − L score is calculated only when both configurations have successful finite scores and pass the same P2Rank/pLDDT thresholds. Best mode selects L and D best pockets independently; control-matched mode requires both scores at the same L-derived experimental-site-matched pocket and receptor geometry, with no fallback. Positive, negative and exactly zero differences count as L preferred, D preferred and tied. Percentages divide by the number of evaluable pairs for that metric; missing pairs are reported separately. Neither D availability nor L preference changes L recovery ranks, curve denominators or experimental-site recovery. D-AA results never add pockets to the L-derived control-site reference. The audit table and TSV include metric-specific D scores, D pocket IDs and deltas. These comparisons measure predicted stereochemical preference, not established nonbinding, true affinity or an empirical FDR.

Only pockets retained in successful docking results are accessible here. This is a residue-overlap diagnostic, not complete P2Rank detection sensitivity and not a ligand-to-pocket-center distance test. Full evaluation would also need the unfiltered P2Rank outputs and experimentally aligned structures. TSV exports identify the mode, matching threshold, match status, per-metric pocket/score/percentile/site coverage, denominator counts and reference convention. See the P2Rank documentation for structure-based evaluation.

Selection and reference population

GO analysis uses Explorer’s Best pocket rule: first apply P2Rank/pLDDT thresholds to every successful finite-score pocket, then choose the lowest passing score per protein for the target AA and assign its percentile within that QC-qualified population. The selected group additionally passes the top-tier cutoff, Competitive AAs maximum and L>D when enabled. The default Parameter-filtered reference contains all proteins passing the same P2Rank/pLDDT thresholds, without the tier, Competitive AAs or L>D cutoff. Total (target AA) instead includes all proteins with a successful finite score for the selected AA and metric, ignoring quality thresholds; it does not include proteins missing that AA score or annotation-only proteins. Both references include the selected group, and changing background does not change those selected hits. Failed protein × AA results never remove successful results for another AA. D-AA controls are not targets in this tab.

The protein-selection controls are independent of Explorer; “Use Explorer settings” also copies the Competitive AAs limit and L>D, but not search, other specificity filters or All pockets mode. Defaults: Alanine, Top 5%, Combined 50%, P2Rank ≥0.7, pLDDT ≥90 and Competitive AAs ≤19. GO tiers are Top 1%, 5%, 10% and 20%–100% in 10% steps. Explorer All ranks maps to Top 100%; copying Explorer settings preserves the GO background choice, aspect and FDR threshold. At Top 100% with no competitor restriction and L>D off, the filtered selection equals its background, so fold enrichment and P/FDR are 1. Total background can still differ because of the quality filters.
Annotation coverage and counting

The local UniProt GO file supplies BP (Biological Process), MF (Molecular Function) and CC (Cellular Component) mappings. A protein is counted once per GO term; duplicate rows and repeated terms are merged. For each aspect, selected and reference proteins without any annotation in that aspect are excluded from its tests, but remain visible in the selected-protein list. Coverage reports annotated / total proteins.

Only terms explicitly supplied in the file are analyzed; no ancestor propagation, GO-slim conversion, evidence-code filtering or live annotation update is performed. The unit is a UniProt accession, not a collapsed gene locus; isoforms and related proteins may not be independent.
Fisher’s exact test: counts and effect size

Selected k/n: k selected proteins annotated to this term, out of n selected proteins annotated in its aspect. Background K/N uses the same definition for the reference population. Expected hits = n × K/N. Fold enrichment above 1 means the term is more frequent in the selection. P-value is the one-sided Fisher exact (hypergeometric upper-tail) probability of at least k hits under random selection from that reference.

Fold = (k / n) / (K / N) · P = Pr[X ≥ k], X ~ Hypergeometric(N, K, n)Background counts include the selected proteins. No depletion test is performed. Log-space calculations preserve very small probabilities.
FDR, plot and downloads

Benjamini–Hochberg correction is applied jointly across all background GO terms from all three aspects, including terms with zero selected hits. GO aspect (default Biological process) and FDR threshold (default 0.05) are in the main options row. An enriched term has fold >1 and FDR at or below the chosen threshold. The table’s FDR-only checkbox is checked and active by default; uncheck it to see other terms with selected hits. Changing aspect, threshold, search or display filters does not recalculate FDR or change the protein populations. Changing background does recalculate counts, P-values and BH FDR across the chosen background’s terms. The chart shows up to 12 enriched terms ranked by adjusted significance, with fold enrichment on the x-axis and a purple color gradient for −log10(FDR). Darker bars indicate smaller FDR; the legend runs from −log10(the chosen threshold) to the largest displayed −log10(FDR), with a minimum span of 0.1.

Click k/n to inspect its selected proteins. TSV/CSV term downloads include all matching rows, not just the page, plus counts, settings (including the FDR threshold), hit IDs, P/FDR and their −log10 values. Separate TSV downloads contain the displayed protein set or the complete chosen background, including unannotated proteins. All table exports record background_mode; plot exports also label the background. Docking enrichment is descriptive, not proof of binding or causation; repeated exploration and related GO terms warrant caution.

Competitive AAs in the selected-protein table: uses Explorer’s score-tolerance rule with the GO tab’s target AA and ranking score. Counts other canonical AAs whose best successful score is ≤ target score + tolerance (Combined 80%/50%: 0.10 each; Vina: 0.25; SFCT: 0.15). Shown as count / available other AAs, up to 19; target AA and D-AA controls are excluded. Missing scores are unknown, not noncompetitive. Other-AA scores pass the same GO P2Rank/pLDDT thresholds before best-pocket selection, but are not filtered by GO tier. The Competitive AAs ≤ filter applies this count to the selected proteins before GO enrichment, without restricting the chosen background. Default 19 is unrestricted; lower values require an available comparison. This differs from Top-hit overlap’s x/20 membership count. Protein TSV downloads include the count, available-other-AA denominator, tolerance and configured maximum; term downloads also record the maximum.
